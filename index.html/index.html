About Us

Sempadan Asasi Sdn Bhd is a Malaysia-based agricultural company focused on producing high-quality palm oil fresh fruit bunches (FFB). Our operations combine field expertise with sustainable estate management to serve downstream processors and long-term partners.

Our Mission: To support the palm oil value chain with reliable fruit production, responsible land stewardship, and continuous improvement in quality and efficiency.
